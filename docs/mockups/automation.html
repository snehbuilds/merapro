Modes

Manual

Every post waits for a human. The default, and the only mode enabled in Phase 1.

Assisted

Publishes unattended above a confidence threshold with every guardrail clear. Everything else goes to the bot.

Full autopilot

End to end within guardrails: daily caps, allowed topics, quiet hours, blocked keywords. Optional notice in the bot.

Guardrails

Shifts timers rather than consuming them — a reminder due at 02:00 fires at 07:00 and expiry moves with it.
Require staggering within a group
Blocks "all at once" when two accounts share a platform
Approval for deletions
Applies even in Full autopilot

Kill switch

Stops ingestion, agent runs, scheduling and publishing immediately. Pending approvals are cancelled; queued posts are held. Nothing is lost.
